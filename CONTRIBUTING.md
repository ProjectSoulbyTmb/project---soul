# Contribution policy

Project Soul is not accepting source-code contributions unless the repository owner first approves them in writing and supplies an appropriate contribution or assignment agreement.

Issues may describe bugs or user-visible feature requests, but contributors should not submit confidential information, patent-sensitive technical disclosures, proprietary code, or third-party material they do not have authority to provide. Opening an issue does not transfer ownership of the submission.

Pull requests submitted without prior written approval may be closed without review or incorporation. This policy helps keep inventorship, ownership, licensing, and development provenance unambiguous.
