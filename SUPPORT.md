# Support

Eidovara is independent, source-available software for adults 18 or older.

## Where to get help

- **Documentation:** <https://eidovara.org/> (Product, Download, Help, FAQ, Status, Legal)
- **In-app:** Settings -> Help, the keyboard cheatsheet (`Ctrl+/`), and Diagnostics
- **Bug reports & feature requests:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ProjectSoulbyTmb/project---soul/issues)
- **Security vulnerabilities:** please use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ProjectSoulbyTmb/project---soul/security/advisories/new) - do not open public issues for security problems. See also [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and our [security policy](https://github.com/ProjectSoulbyTmb/project---soul/security/policy).

## What support is

- Best-effort community help from the maintainer, in English
- Guidance on installation on Windows 10/11 x64, updates, backups, and documented features

## What support is not

- Not professional, medical, therapeutic, or crisis support. Soul is a software self-model - not a person and not a claim of consciousness.
- Not guaranteed-response commercial support. There are no paid SLAs; payments are disabled.
- Not legal advice. See [TERMS](TERMS.md), [PRIVACY](PRIVACY.md), and [LEGAL NOTICES](LEGAL_NOTICES.md).

## Scope

- Official platform: Windows 10/11 x64. Linux/macOS scripts are development targets only.
- The current release line is v1.0.0 (Stable Alpha). Older pages in the version history remain for reference.
