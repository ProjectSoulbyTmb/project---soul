The product is the Windows app, not this page.

Eidovara v0.22.2 is the current published desktop release. Install Eidovara-0.22.2-Windows-x64-Setup.exe to use the workspace on your PC. The installer is Authenticode-unsigned and has a measured SHA-256 plus GitHub/Sigstore provenance.

Apps & gaming

Discover local Start Menu shortcuts, organize trusted applications, confirm launches, and use low-overhead gaming tools without process injection or anti-cheat interaction.

Media & entertainment

Play user-selected local files and properly sourced public audio or video, keep queues and favorites, and open official HTTPS media searches. Playback controls remain user-directed.

Internet research

On explicit internet requests, retrieve cited and sanitized public information, images, audio, or video where supported, with fail-closed URL handling and source-aware presentation.

Private continuity

Keep reviewable preferences, conversations, backups, appearance, and workspace configuration local by default, with persistent continuity and recovery support.

Optional Soul

Configurable tone, memory, continuity, voice, presence, and initiative—or use the workspace without a remote model. Soul is software, not a claim of sentience.

Online status & updates

The desktop can use the official Eidovara service for health/status/config, check GitHub Releases for updates, verify release metadata, and keep working locally if online services are unavailable.
