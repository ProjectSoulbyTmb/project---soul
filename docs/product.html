What ships in the desktop workspace.

These modules exist in the advertised Windows app. This page is not a roadmap of vapor features. Soul is optional. Neural TTS, VRM, MakeHuman, and OBS websocket control are not bundled.

Apps

Discover local Start Menu shortcuts, organize trusted .exe/.lnk files, confirm on screen, then ask Windows to launch them. Free keeps up to three linked apps. You must already have the right to use those applications. Eidovara does not inject into processes.

Gaming

Low-overhead gaming mode reduces Eidovara visuals only. It does not promise higher frame rates, universal game compatibility, or anti-cheat cooperation. No process injection.

Entertainment

Play user-selected local files and properly sourced public audio or video. Keep queues and favorites. Open official Spotify and YouTube HTTPS searches. Playback can require confirmation. Streams are not ripped.

Research

On an explicit internet, web, or online request, retrieve cited Wikipedia/Wikimedia results, including pictures, audio, and video when asked, or open secure pages in the system browser. Broad keyed Brave Search is a Premium test gate with a key you supply.

Optional Soul

Configurable tone, focus, memory, voice, avatar, and initiative. Free / Offline Soul works with no cloud URL. Soul is software assistance—not therapy, not a human, and not a consciousness claim. This website helper is a separate FAQ widget, not desktop Soul.

Backups

Preferences, conversations, appearance, and workspace configuration stay local by default. Encrypted settings and backups use Windows credential protection when available. Review or remove local data from Settings.

Honest limits

Official advertised platform is Windows only. Linux/macOS scripts are development targets. Installers are Authenticode-unsigned. Premium in v0.18.0 is local-admin testing only. Eidovara is not an iOS or iPhone product and does not require licensed SF Pro fonts.
