Frequently asked

Straight answers for v0.18.2.

Not legal advice. The Ask Eidovara helper uses the same facts.

What is Eidovara?

A local-first Windows 10/11 x64 desktop workspace for apps, gaming tools, media, research, backups, and optional Soul. Visiting this website does not create an account. Soul is optional software, not a consciousness claim.

How do I install Eidovara?

Confirm 18+ on Download, then get unsigned Eidovara-0.18.2-Windows-x64-Setup.exe (about 101.3 MiB). SHA-256 EF228574DCDF34B8A9039654F2B762FAB6D289CCA9A94B2ECCF048AE971FE711. Or build on Windows with npm run dist:win:installer.

Why does Windows warn me?

Official advertised installers are Authenticode-unsigned: not Microsoft-certified. SmartScreen may warn. Verify the SHA-256 on Download.

Is there a Linux, macOS, or iOS app?

No official Linux or macOS product. Not an iOS, iPhone, iPad, or Apple product.

Do I need the cloud?

No. Free / Offline Soul works with no Worker URL. After 18+, Settings → Eidovara service can call /health, /v1/config, and /v1/status only. Conversations are not sent.

Can I pay for Premium here?

No. v0.18.2 does not sell Premium and does not process payments.

Is the website helper Soul?

No. Ask Eidovara is a website helper over a fixed knowledge pack. Not Soul and not legal advice. Transcripts are not stored.
