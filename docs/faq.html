Frequently asked

Straight answers for v0.18.2.

These answers match the advertised Windows product. They are not legal advice. Use Ask Eidovara for the same knowledge pack.

What is Eidovara?

A local-first Windows 10/11 x64 desktop workspace for apps, gaming tools, media, research, backups, and optional Soul. Visiting this website does not create an account. Soul is optional software, not a consciousness claim.

How do I install Eidovara?

Confirm you are 18 or older on the Download page. The primary action there downloads the official unsigned installer Eidovara-0.18.2-Windows-x64-Setup.exe — the .exe, not the GitHub source tree. SHA-256 is on the Download page. You can still build on Windows with npm run dist:win:installer. Adults 18+.

Why does Windows warn me?

Official advertised installers are Authenticode-unsigned. They are not Microsoft-certified, EV-signed, or SmartScreen-preapproved. We cannot Authenticode-sign until the owner provides a certificate. SmartScreen may warn. Verify SHA-256 and GitHub provenance. Provenance is not Authenticode.

Is there a Linux, macOS, or iOS app?

No official Linux or macOS product. Not an iOS, iPhone, iPad, or Apple product. Linux/macOS scripts are development targets.

Do I need the cloud?

No. Free / Offline Soul works with no Worker URL. After 18+, Settings → Eidovara service can call /health, /v1/config, and /v1/status only. Conversations are not sent.

Can I pay for Premium here?

No. v0.18.2 does not sell Premium and does not process payments. Ctrl+A local-admin testing is not checkout or PCI processing.

Is the website helper Soul?

No. Ask Eidovara is a website helper over a fixed knowledge pack. It is not Soul and not legal advice. Transcripts are not stored.

Where are the legal documents?

Start at the legal hub for Terms, Privacy, Age 18+, Licensing, and Security. Repository notices are not a clearance opinion. Contributor and entity assignment files are unsigned templates only.

Why would the live website look older than git?

GitHub Pages publishes docs/ from main only. This site is that live github.io homepage after PR #10 merged. Later branches do not appear here until they merge to main. Do not retarget Pages at a feature branch.
