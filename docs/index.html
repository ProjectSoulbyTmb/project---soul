Three reasons to install

Useful with or without a connected assistant.

Local Windows workspace

Apps, gaming tools, backups, settings, and trusted launch controls stay on your PC. No process injection or anti-cheat interaction.

Media and research

Play user-selected local files and properly sourced public audio or video. Retrieve cited and sanitized information on explicit internet requests.

Optional Soul

Use configurable tone, memory, continuity, voice, presence, and initiative—or use the workspace without a remote model. Soul is software, not a consciousness claim.

Trust by design

Accurate boundaries, visible controls.

Eidovara uses visible permissions, HTTPS handoffs, update hashes, provenance records, local-first storage, and fail-closed network handling. Official installers are Authenticode-unsigned Windows builds. Source-available, not open source. Eidovara is not an iOS or iPhone product and is not affiliated with Apple.
